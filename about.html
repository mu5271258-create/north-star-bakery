About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighbourhood bakery focused on providing fresh baked goods to the local community.

We welcome families, commuters, local customers and people planning special events.

Our Philosophy

We focus on carefully prepared breads, pastries and cakes while providing friendly service to our customers.

Our bakery team works together to prepare fresh baked goods for our customers and community.
